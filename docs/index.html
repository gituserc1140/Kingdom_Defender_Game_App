Kingdom Defender

Defend your castle from endless waves.

How to Play

  • Recruit defenders to stop enemies moving from right to left.
  • Use gold from enemy defeats to recruit and upgrade.
  • Unlock Knight at wave 3, Mage at wave 5.
  • Damage/Range/Repair upgrades unlock at wave 10.
  • Elite enemies start at wave 20, boss appears every 10 waves.
